## 2. Description of Service

### 2.1 What EyeRhythm Does
EyeRhythm is a desktop application that monitors your blink rate using your device's camera to help you maintain healthy eye habits during screen time. The Service:
- Processes video from your camera locally on your device
- Detects and tracks blink patterns in real-time
- Provides fatigue alerts and break reminders
- Stores session data and analytics

### 2.2 Privacy-First Approach
All video processing occurs entirely on your device. Your camera feed is never transmitted, recorded, or stored. Only derived metrics (blink timestamps, session duration, etc.) are optionally synced to our servers if you create an account.

### 2.3 Not Medical Advice
**Important:** EyeRhythm is a wellness tool designed to promote healthy screen time habits. It is NOT a medical device and does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The Service should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice. If you have concerns about your eye health, please consult a qualified healthcare professional.
