## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does the free trial work?">
    EyeRhythm doesn't have a traditional free trial. Instead, we offer a generous free tier that you can use forever. The free tier includes all core features with 7 days of data history. Upgrade to Premium anytime to unlock 365-day history and advanced features.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription anytime?">
    Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings or through the Stripe billing portal. When you cancel, you'll retain Premium access until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account will automatically revert to the free tier.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I cancel?">
    When you downgrade to the free tier, your data history is limited to the most recent 7 days. Data older than 7 days will be automatically deleted. We recommend exporting your data before cancelling if you want to keep historical records.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer refunds?">
    We offer refunds on a case-by-case basis. If you're not satisfied with Premium within the first 7 days, contact us at benedict@eyerhythm.com and we'll be happy to discuss a refund. After 7 days, we generally don't offer refunds, but you can always cancel to prevent future charges.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) through our secure payment processor, Stripe. We also support Apple Pay and Google Pay where available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my payment information secure?">
    Absolutely. All payment processing is handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. EyeRhythm never stores or has access to your full card details. Your payment information is encrypted and handled entirely by Stripe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch between monthly and yearly plans?">
    Yes! You can switch plans at any time from your billing portal. If you switch from monthly to yearly, you'll receive credit for any unused time. If you switch from yearly to monthly, the change will take effect at the end of your current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer student or non-profit discounts?">
    We don't currently offer formal discount programmes, but we're always happy to discuss individual circumstances. Contact us at benedict@eyerhythm.com if you'd like to discuss your situation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What currency are prices in?">
    All prices are listed in British Pounds (GBP). If you're paying from outside the UK, your bank or card provider will convert the charge to your local currency at their prevailing exchange rate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I manage my subscription?">
    You can manage your subscription directly from the EyeRhythm app. Go to Settings, then Subscription to access the Stripe billing portal where you can update payment methods, change plans, view invoices, or cancel your subscription.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
