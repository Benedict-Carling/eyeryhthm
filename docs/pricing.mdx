## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How does the free trial work?">
    There's no trial period - the Free plan is free forever! You can use EyeRhythm's core features without ever paying. Premium simply adds extended history, cloud sync, and advanced features for power users.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I cancel my subscription?">
    Yes, you can cancel anytime from your account settings. When you cancel, you'll keep Premium access until the end of your current billing period. After that, you'll automatically move to the Free plan and retain your last 7 days of data.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my data if I downgrade?">
    When you downgrade from Premium to Free, your session history will be limited to the most recent 7 days. Older sessions will be archived but not deleted - if you upgrade again within 30 days, your full history will be restored.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my payment information secure?">
    Yes. We use Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor, to handle all payments. We never see or store your full card details. Stripe is trusted by millions of businesses worldwide including Google, Amazon, and Shopify.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer refunds?">
    We offer a full refund within the first 7 days of your initial Premium subscription if you're not satisfied. After that, you can cancel anytime but refunds are not provided for partial billing periods. Contact us at benedict@eyerhythm.com for refund requests.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I switch between monthly and yearly?">
    Yes! You can switch from monthly to yearly (or vice versa) at any time from your account settings. When switching to yearly, you'll be charged the annual rate immediately and receive credit for any unused time on your monthly plan.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a student or educational discount?">
    We're planning to introduce educational pricing in the future. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when it becomes available.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods do you accept?">
    We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) through Stripe. We also support Apple Pay and Google Pay where available.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
